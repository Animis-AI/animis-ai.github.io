We build the world engine for physical AI.

Robots learn in simulation before they act in the world. Animis AI turns the physical world into its simulatable twin — assets with articulation, mass, friction and collision, generated at scale and validated in physics engines — so embodied intelligence has a world to train in.

The world engine

Three layers, one loop: capture the world, make it simulatable, and prove it behaves — then feed it to robot learning at whatever scale training demands.

01

Sim-ready generation

From a single image to a simulatable asset: part-level geometry, PBR materials, convex collision and a URDF that loads straight into MuJoCo, Isaac and Genesis.

02

Articulation & physics

Recovered joint axes and motion limits, measured mass and friction, soft-body and cloth solvers — every parameter probed in a physics engine, not asserted.

03

Scale & audit

Batch pipelines that texture, decompose and validate thousands of objects — with quality gates and white-box reports at every stage, so scale never outruns correctness.
